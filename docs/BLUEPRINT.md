What comes next, the route there, what is undecided, and a compact record of how the system arrived. `ARCHITECTURE.md` (beside this file) is the current shape and its limitations; this file is direction and the measurements the architecture map does not carry. ## 1. Next session — S15 Authority II: consent on the record

The first open entry of the route in §2, expanded. **Purpose:** a person consents to what the RUNTIME says a call will do, not to the model's account of it — and a decision can outlive one call without outliving its session.

**Step 0.** `ARCHITECTURE.md` is at 97% of its ceiling and cannot absorb another session's contracts (§4, S14). Settle the budget before writing a line of it: either the ceiling moves (CLAUDE.md §5's own rule says it sits just ABOVE what the rules measure, and they now measure ~84 KB) or §13 and §12 move out to a document of their own. This is a CLAUDE.md change and needs the user. Then read `references/dsh/authority.md`, and research only DSH's approval subject and grant paths.

**Work, in order.** (1) A requester-written structured `subject` on `ApprovalRequest` and `approval/asked`, from S14's effect vocabulary — the "about to do" half beside the "did" half — control-stripped and clamped by the seam. (2) `OpenApproval` carries `callId` and the joined call; both clients render the FULL call, never an 80-character preview; decided-by provenance. (3) **Shell-enforcement acceptance** as its own knob (ARCHITECTURE §11 "an authority knob": a log-only fact, a fold, an opening stamp): the PERSISTENT shell runs unconfined while the in-process fs fence stays, copied down at delegation (narrowing only), dropped on a resume that finds a confining host. That is the Windows fix. (4) Session-lifetime **grants** as an op-shaped log-only fold consulted inside `ApprovalService.request` where `never` is enforced: identity = tool + exact subject; a hit still writes the asked/decided pair with a `grantId`; scopes are offered BY THE HOST; never across a delegation ceiling; revocable. (5) Enforcement-aware first-ask guidance on every surface, a deterministic headless allow-list, provider credentials scrubbed from the shell child, and the network stance: egress is outside the file stamp, a network tool is an opt-in authority-sensitive row tagged `NETWORK`.

**Preserve.** `allowed-once` the only granting outcome; one decision per ask; `never` before dispatch; durable ceilings and pins; no surface invents a scope; no service writes model-facing prose; a subject is derived by trusted code from validated arguments, never model text — upstream cannot prove its approved arguments are the executed ones, because its three waterfalls may rewrite a call between the logged `tool/call` and the body, and MiniDSH should not inherit that.

**Not.** A Windows native backend, prefix or pattern grants (they need a per-dialect parser that refuses compound forms; DSH has none), workspace-lifetime grants, model-based approval, a network sandbox.

**Gate** (an ordinary session, CLAUDE.md §10): `pnpm check` on Windows and under WSL 2 with both `MINIDSH_EXPECT_*` flags; the `authority` arc (its escalation leg IS the change) and `delegation` (a child's pin must still refuse), once each; a bounded review.

**The ordering principle: deepen the record before widening the runtime.** Every later capability adds a new kind of effect or a new concurrent actor, and each must be expressible as log facts, under recorded authority, with a recovery rule, so those three come first. One concept runs through the first cluster: a core **effect vocabulary** (what a call is about to do and what it did, written by trusted code from validated arguments, never model prose) with three consumers: the crash record (S14), the consent subject and grants (S15), the audit of effects (S16). Authority precedes jobs because on Windows a background job cannot exist while every shell call is a one-shot escalation. S18 and S19 need no new seam and may be pulled earlier on demand. **Checkpoints follow clusters of architectural change**, never every session: the record and authority planes; the widened runtime; multi-actor and product. Each `.5` does what CLAUDE.md §6 says. S16.5 also takes what S13 parked: publishing from CI (OIDC trusted publishing from a `release.yml` on a `v*` tag with `--provenance`, which also removes the interactive-2FA step), and the routing arc's TWO failure shapes reported separately: the marker drop with `history_read` called 0×, and the model going off-instruction with the information reachable, which is a prompt problem, not a compaction problem.

**S16 — integrity and forensics.** *Does:* the format evolution rule (a per-lifecycle format and provenance fact honoured as max-over-log; `freezeEnvelope` refuses unknown envelope fields; an unknown kind carrying `surfaceOp` is a clean version refusal); checkpoint durability MEASURED (`fdatasync` at checkpoints, or narrow the claim); `minidsh sessions inspect|verify <id>` as cold reads that never take the lease (timeline with brackets, would-be closers, open approvals and grants, the queued inbox, lineage, effects, cost; an exported pure trace validator; damage diagnosis; an exit code); an audit of attempts and effects, not only decisions; `capabilities/llm-replay` promoted from test-support, effect-free through a `tools/execute` around-middleware; the demo log as a keyless replay fixture in `pnpm check`; fork from a damaged log's readable prefix. *Keeps:* reads never lock or repair; committed bytes are never rewritten; 1.0.0 logs stay readable. *Not:* a hash chain without measured need, deletion, search, a projection store, a log-viewer UI.

**S17 — jobs.** *Does:* a `core/jobs` Definition (a 19th key) shipping its driver. A job is owned by an agent scope (one `ctx.effect`), has a `job/start|end` bracket that may span turns with an exactly-once terminal fact, completes ONLY through the durable inbox (busy: next step; idle: a bounded waking insert whose budget only user input refills), is bounded by REFUSAL at start, and closes `lost` when its host died (S14's closers; no re-adoption). First producer: a handle-returning verb on the `shell` Definition built from the existing one-shot child (own process group, reaped at disposal), output to spill. Tools `job_start`, `job_output`, `job_cancel`; `SessionView.jobs` folded from the log; a cancel verb; the deployment-scope control-plane subscription (a summary push that ignores narrowing, answerer candidacy tied to it); the headless rule for a live job at `whenIdle`. DSH's jobs are in-memory and leave no session events: MiniDSH diverges on purpose. *Keeps:* the inbox is the only queue; a producer may not start work its owner cannot collect; a job runs under the session stamp and S15's acceptance, never a one-shot escalation; output is never trace events. *Not:* a scheduler, retries, dependencies, mailboxes, a generic task supertype, parallel tool calls.

**S18 — providers and the first network tool (movable).** `capabilities/llm-openai` on the Responses API (encrypted reasoning and item ids as the `ReplayEnvelope`; unsupported options refused before I/O; honest duplication of the HTTP plumbing); the routing arc gains an OpenAI leg; DeepSeek's unknown-id facts made conservative and its model-id rename absorbed; `capabilities/tool-web-search` (Tavily) as an OPT-IN row (`CredentialRef`, `NETWORK` consent, structured sources only, spill, a fixed untrusted-content notice); `web_fetch` only behind a full SSRF guard, or not at all. *Not:* generic OpenAI-compatible routing (when a third chat-completions provider is wanted, with facts from row config), a fetched catalog, a shared wire package in core.

**S19 — skills as data (movable).** After S15 because a document skill runs scripts through the shell, which Windows can only use freely once acceptance exists. One `capabilities/skills` row; discovery roots as row config from `app/home.ts`; the catalog is ONE durable plugin-sourced `user/message` (the workspace-instructions pattern: a non-empty first batch only, re-entered after a compaction shadows it), listing name, description, absolute path and origin class; bodies load through the existing editor or shell read, so no skill tool and no log kind; front-matter beyond name and description is ignored; a workspace skill cannot shadow by name (DSH gives workspace skills top precedence with no trust gate: a warning). docx, pdf, xlsx and pptx as DATA: zero-dependency `.mjs` helpers plus host runtimes probed, degrading by stating what was not verified; a packaging assertion; a live arc asserting the WORLD. *Not:* a marketplace, skills registering tools or hooks, per-skill authority or model, a PDF attachment kind, a watcher.

**S20 — browser verification.** `capabilities/tool-browser`: a hand-written CDP client over `--remote-debugging-pipe` to an installed Chrome or Edge, a functional probe at mount, the process owned by the live agent scope and never restored from the log; text tools first; screenshots through a core image-admission helper moved out of `tool-view-image` (step 0); the verifier child via `model-roles`; the dev server as an S17 job; a private profile, downloads off, the environment scrubbed, no loopback debug port a confined shell could reach; `MINIDSH_EXPECT_BROWSER`. *Not:* a `core/browser` Definition before a second provider, Playwright, computer use, a tool running its own model without a durable `llm/aux-call`.

**S21 — more than one actor.** Step 0: replay keyed by `subagent/start{callId, childId}`, never first-request order. A continuable child is a durable child session, a resident activation and the child's own inbox (no jobs, as upstream); `send` and `interrupt` (cancel keeping the inbox); `ResumeAgentOptions.setup` so narrowing survives a resume; runtime-authored notices under a distinct source kind; the one-shot background child as the second job kind; parallel tool calls through a per-call, pure, fail-closed `isConcurrencySafe(args)` with results committed in model order. *Not:* agent teams, mailboxes, schedulers, swarm presets.

**S22 — surfaces.** `session/fork` and resume-without-prompt on the wire, the terminal's beside-the-wire bypass deleted; `publishView` on a status change; a graceful web stop and `web --json`; a terminal line editor and status line; browser markdown, cards, a model picker, uploads and attachment reads; tool KIND on the wire so no surface hard-codes a tool name; Desktop as a packaged launcher plus a webview over the existing carrier; the importable-surface decision; the remaining visual assets (a terminal recording, a browser screenshot, a social preview). *Not:* a second runtime, surface-held state.

**Still refused, with the reason:** model-written extensions (upstream replaced its own with gated plugin installs; `node:vm` is no boundary); an MCP bridge (no demonstrated need); a Windows confinement backend (native bindings, standing ACL changes, `partial` today) and Landlock (a prebuilt native launcher); session deletion (no authority above the append-only seam, none upstream); PTC; a session search index; per-user identity; agent teams.

## 3. Open questions (a decision is pending; the limitations themselves live in ARCHITECTURE §13)

- **What does a resumed session learn about a `lost` job, and how does replay order a completion's arrival?** Upstream has no oracle (its jobs leave no events). S17 decides both.
- **What is `ARCHITECTURE.md` for, now that its rules measure more than its ceiling?** S14 left it at 97%: three independent compressors at binding maxima freed 0.37%, and one session of contracts added 6.7 KB. Either the ceiling moves (CLAUDE.md §5 sets it just ABOVE what the rules measure) or §12 and §13 become a document of their own. A CLAUDE.md change either way, so S15 step 0 asks.
- **A second surface writer.** Only the compaction planner enforces "a replace never separates a call from its result", and the session invariant admits a `replace`-op `user/message` outside a step but not a `tool/result`. A pruner would be that second writer; the rule moves into the session when one exists.
- **Workspace-lifetime grants.** A grant that outlives a session cannot live in one log, `settings` may not own authority, and no workspace layer exists: a home-side store keyed by canonical root, COPIED into opening facts, is the only shape that keeps a stored log's audit self-contained.
- **A document input kind.** A PDF block is a vocabulary change (`AttachmentRef`, `ModelModality`, `ContentBlock`, both serializers, the meter); upstream's generic file kind, projected as one handle line and never bytes, gives every model PDF support for nothing.
- **What projection would a rename need?** `session/title` is last-wins, but nothing reads the CURRENT title cheaply: a name chosen at seq 5,000 is nowhere near the bounded prefix `persistence.list()` reads. A sidecar, a writer-maintained stamp, or a real projection store (upstream mounts one by default).
- **Two provider facts to design with.** DeepSeek's `user_id` gives each id its own rate budget, a seam for session identity; Anthropic caps a request at 32 MB with a 413 that maps to `INVALID_REQUEST`, which nothing retries and nothing compacts.
- **Does MiniDSH publish an importable surface?** A module-loaded plugin's own imports do not resolve from an installed package. An `exports` map is a compatibility promise; the first out-of-tree plugin decides, or S22 if its launcher hosts the runtime in-process rather than over the carrier. Skills do not need it.
- **How does a provider catalog stay current?** S11.5 found five Anthropic ids no prefix matched; on 2026-09-17 DeepSeek's `GET /models` listed `deepseek-flash` and `deepseek-v4-pro` while `deepseek-v4-flash` still answered. A snapshot in source, or fetched and cached: S18 meets it.

## 4. Development record

One entry per session: what it decided, and the measurements that still matter. The detail lives in the commits.

- **S14 Durable execution I (2026-09-20).** **What it decided:** the DRIVER writes the gate-to-body fact, not the pipeline — a design review caught that a registry-returned `DURABILITY_LOST` RESULT never reaches the loop's lost-write state machine, so a last call that concludes the turn could close it `completed` after a lost write; driver ownership also let `tool/dispatch` join `STEP_SCOPED` at no test cost. It is awaited inside the `tools/execute` TERMINAL continuation, because S16's replay middleware will replace bodies there. The compaction closer says `unclosed`, not `interrupted`: an unpaired start is produced by ordinary disposal as often as by a crash. Effects are folded per STEP, because nothing makes a call id unique across steps. **The compatibility row is load-bearing:** a log that records no dispatch may be a 1.0.0 one, so absence is only evidence in a log that has shown it records them — one hole, stated, that S16's provenance fact closes. **Found on the way:** a disposed shell answered like a command that ran and printed nothing. **What DSH research changed:** nothing structural, but three justifications — upstream puts the same fail-closed barrier in the same place and writes no fact; its `tool/ptc-dispatch-start` is a near-identical name for the OPPOSITE side of a gate; it has no `subagent` bracket at all, and declines the child→parent call join outright. **The measurement that matters:** `ARCHITECTURE.md` cannot take another session (§3). 736 tests; 4 research + 3 compaction agents.
